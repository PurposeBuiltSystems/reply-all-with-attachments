Reply All with Attachments

Reply to everyone and keep the original attachments — the ones Outlook silently throws away.

One extra ribbon button Custom reply signature No data collection Free

Why

Outlook strips attachments when you Reply All. That's sensible when the attachment is a 40 MB deck nobody needs bounced around a thread — and wrong whenever the attachment is the conversation. A contract everyone is marking up. A drawing under review. A form being passed along. Then you're forwarding instead, re-adding six recipients by hand, and hoping you didn't miss one.

What it does

  • Replies to everyone, attachments intact — one button in the Reply All + ribbon group.
  • Leaves it as a draft — you read it and press Send. Nothing is ever sent for you.
  • Adds your signature — set it once, including an image; these replies are built by the add-in, so Outlook's own signature doesn't apply to them.
  • Doesn't duplicate inline images — pictures embedded in the message body stay in the quoted reply and aren't attached a second time.

Privacy by design

No servers, no accounts, no analytics. It runs in your own Outlook session against Microsoft Graph — inside the Microsoft 365 boundary. We never see your email. Read the privacy policy.

Get it

Available on Microsoft AppSource. In Outlook, open Get Add-ins and search for Reply All with Attachments. Questions: Matthew@purposebuilt.systems.
