This signature is added to the top of every Reply All with Attachments reply.

  1. Open an email that already shows your signature (one you sent works great), or Outlook Settings → Mail.
  2. Select your whole signature — logo and all — and copy it (⌘C / Ctrl+C).
  3. Click the box below and paste (⌘V / Ctrl+V). The text and, usually, the logo come in together.
  4. If the logo is missing or shows a broken icon: in the sent email, right-click the logo → Save Image As…, then click Add image… below and pick that file.
  5. Click Save. The logo is resized and stored automatically.

📌 Heads-up: when you use Reply All with Attachments, the logo may show as a broken-image icon in the draft you're composing. That's just the editor — the logo will appear correctly once the email is sent.

Images are added on their own line at the end of your signature. To place one somewhere else, copy it and paste at the cursor instead.
